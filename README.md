==================================================

BRAND POSITIONING

==================================================

West Shore Yacht Center is a boutique, full-service marina located in Essex, Maryland, on Back River with convenient access to the Chesapeake Bay.

The marina specializes in premium boat lift-slip rentals for recreational powerboat and yacht owners.

Boats can be kept elevated out of the water when not in use, providing better protection, cleanliness, convenience, and easier boating access.

The marina accommodates boats requiring lifts from approximately 8,000 to 20,000 lbs.

Amenities and services include:

• Premium boat lift-slip rentals

• Lift slips for approximately 8,000–20,000 lb boats

• Water

• Electricity

• Finger-pier access

• Seasonal storage

• Winter storage

• Waterfront access

• Swimming pool

• Outdoor gathering areas

• Grilling areas

• Family-friendly marina atmosphere

• Convenient access to the Chesapeake Bay

• Marina tours

• Slip availability inquiries

Location:

Essex, Maryland

Back River

Approximately 3 miles from the Chesapeake Bay

PRIMARY AUDIENCE:

Recreational powerboat owners

Yacht owners

Families who enjoy Chesapeake Bay boating

Boat owners in Baltimore and surrounding Maryland communities

Boat owners looking for convenient seasonal storage

Boat owners looking to keep their vessels protected out of the water

Owners seeking a boutique marina experience instead of a large commercial marina

PRIMARY WEBSITE GOALS:

1. Generate lift-slip inquiries

2. Generate marina tour requests

3. Encourage visitors to inquire about availability

4. Communicate the benefits of keeping boats out of the water

5. Showcase the marina lifestyle

6. Build trust and credibility

7. Make the marina feel premium, welcoming, safe, and established

8. Make contacting the marina extremely easy
